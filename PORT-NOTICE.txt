DiscordLink+ - unofficial Fabric 26.3 text and voice port.
Based on the voice bridging design in codingsushi79/discordlinkplus,
upstream commit 6360549041dfa0158deeed3365fce87fe67a61a8.
Upstream identifies its license as MIT in README.md.
Original upstream source is retained in src/main for attribution/reference,
but is not compiled into the Fabric mod. New implementation: src/fabric.
The port adds group routing, a bot pool, bounded PCM mixing, static SVC
audio channels and server-side lifecycle integration. The text relay ports
public chat and join/leave/death announcements and adds advancement events,
player webhooks, active-skin avatars, and bounded cross-thread delivery.
The Discord account linking gate and remote console are not included.
